Limitations

  • Prices are Yahoo closes without dividends; MarketWatch fills at its own quotes, and net worth there is the reference. Use NET_WORTH_OVERRIDE if they differ.
  • The transaction trail is built from changes in holdings between runs and priced at the close of the run date, not at the execution price.
  • The index weights are those of 12 Nov 2026 and drift with prices. The sleeve is a view on one event and has no history to test.
