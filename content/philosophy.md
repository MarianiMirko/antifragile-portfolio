## What we believe

Markets reward investors who survive. We would rather give up some upside than be forced to sell at the bottom, so we build the portfolio to gain from volatility instead of merely enduring it: quality businesses, real diversification across sectors, and dry powder in cash and gold.

## How we decide

1. **Idea.** The fundamental analyst writes a thesis with a target price, catalysts and a horizon.
2. **Macro check.** The macro analyst confirms the idea fits the current regime.
3. **Risk check.** The risk analyst sizes the position and tests it against the limits below.
4. **Evidence.** The backtesting analyst shows how the buy and sell rule behaved historically.
5. **Decision.** The portfolio manager approves or rejects the trade and records the reason in the trade log.

## Who decides what

| Decision | Owner | Consulted |
|---|---|---|
| Buy or sell a position | Portfolio manager | Fundamental, risk |
| Position size and limits | Risk analyst | Portfolio manager |
| Target weights | Whole team, majority vote | |
| Changing a rule on this page | Whole team, unanimous | |

When we disagree, the owner of the decision decides, and the dissenting view is written in the trade log next to the trade.

## Rebalancing

We compare actual weights with target weights twice a day. When a position drifts beyond the threshold we propose the trades that bring it back; a person approves every order before it is placed.
