October inflation surprised on the upside: headline CPI rose 0.4% on the month (0.3% expected) and core CPI 0.3% (0.2% expected). On 28 October the Fed held rates at 3.75–4.00% in a 10–2 vote, with two members voting for a hike, and a December hike is now widely expected. The 10-year Treasury yield rose above 5.1% and the dollar reached a 19-month high. On 18 November the S&P 500 closed at 7,323.63, below its 200-day moving average and 1.33% under our start level of 7,422.24.

**What it means for us.** Under our benchmark, max(0; S&P 500), the bonus is out of reach whenever the index ends below its start, so losses below zero cost less than missed upside. We kept high beta only for the short IPO window (beta 1.23 from 16 to 19 November) and returned to a replica that follows the index closely afterwards.

**What we are watching.** Three major releases inside the game: nonfarm payrolls (4 December), the FOMC decision (9 December) and November CPI (10 December, the day after the Fed), plus thin liquidity around Thanksgiving (26 November).
