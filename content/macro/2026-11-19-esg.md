Excluding whole sectors such as energy, defence or tobacco would widen our tracking error and put our risk limits at risk, so we follow a materiality-based approach.

**Governance.** Excluding NVIDIA also removes the large-cap name with the highest governance risk this month: its CEO resigned on 12 November, the CFO is interim CEO, and gross-margin guidance was cut to 71–72%. Marek's audit confirms strong returns on capital and conservative leverage for our other core names.

**Environment.** Fossil-fuel exposure stays at index weight (Exxon 1.3%, Chevron 0.8%). Our main environmental risk is the power demand of the hyperscalers' data centres, whose combined AI investment for 2026 is about USD 720–750 billion.

**Social.** Tobacco (Philip Morris 0.6%) and defence (RTX 0.5%) stay at index weight; antitrust, labour and privacy cases against the largest companies are followed as financial risks.

**Built-in upgrades.** Selling Alibaba removed the only Chinese VIE structure from the portfolio, and a switch to the sector-balanced replica would replace Philip Morris and Wells Fargo with NextEra Energy, a clean-energy utility.
