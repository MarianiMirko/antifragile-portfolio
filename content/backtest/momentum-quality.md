**Rule tested.** Hold the 15 highest-quality names in the S&P 100 (return on equity, low leverage), rebalance monthly, and sell any position that falls 15% below its purchase price.

**What we learned.** The stop-loss cut the worst drawdown by about a third and cost roughly half a point of annual return. That trade-off is why the 15% stop-loss is one of our sell criteria.
